Belong

Your workspace,
a little calmer.

Belong sits quietly in your browser and helps you breathe, move, and focus — without sharing your data with anyone.

🔒

Stays on your device

All data is saved locally. Nothing is sent to any server, ever.

👁️

Domain names only

We track which site — never what you read, type, or click.

🗑️

Gone when you go

Uninstall Belong and every byte of your data disappears.

🚫

No ads. No selling.

We don't monetise your attention or your information.

What we need
access to — and why

We only ask for permissions we actually use. Here's every one explained.

🗂️

Storage

Saves your name, points, settings, and session history locally on this device. It never leaves Chrome.

⏰

Alarms

Powers break reminders. The service worker wakes up only when it's time to notify you, then goes back to sleep.

🔔

Notifications

Shows your chosen break reminder as a native browser alert. You can disable this at any time.

📊

Tabs — domain only

Reads the current tab's hostname (e.g. "notion.so") to track time-on-site. Page content is never accessed.
